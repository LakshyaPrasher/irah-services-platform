Clarity & Trust

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the regulatory status of IRAH Services LLP?

A: IRAH Services LLP is an AMFI-Registered Mutual Fund Distributor (ARN registration in process of allotment by AMFI India). Mutual fund transactions will be processed under IRAH's ARN once AMFI allots it. For products such as PMS and AIFs, we facilitate access through arrangements with SEBI-registered Portfolio Managers and fund houses. We do not operate as an Investment Adviser (RIA).

Q: How are client investment funds routed?

A: Client money never passes through IRAH Services LLP, in any account. Payments go from your bank directly to the AMC, or through BSE StAR MF or NSE NMF II, or directly to the PMS or AIF manager's designated account. IRAH does not accept cash.

Q: What is the minimum ticket for PMS and AIF?

A: As per SEBI regulations, Portfolio Management Services (PMS) require a statutory minimum investment of ₹50 Lakhs. Alternative Investment Funds (Category I, II, III AIF) require a minimum commitment of ₹1.00 Crore per investor under the SEBI AIF Regulations, subject to limited SEBI exceptions.

Q: Can IRAH tell me which fund to buy?

A: No. IRAH is not a SEBI-registered Investment Adviser and does not give personalised investment advice. Product choice is yours after reading the scheme documents. For advice, consult a SEBI-registered Investment Adviser.

Q: How is IRAH compensated?

A: By AMCs through commission on Regular plans, and by PMS, AIF, and issuer partners through referral or distribution fees where applicable. IRAH charges clients no advisory or transaction fee. Details are provided in writing on request.

Q: Direct or Regular plan?

A: Direct plans carry no distributor commission and can be bought from the AMC. Regular plans include distributor commission in their expense ratio, from which the distributor's servicing and execution support are funded.

Q: How do I verify IRAH's credentials?

A: Use the AMFI Locate Distributor portal. IRAH's ARN registration is in process of allotment by AMFI India; the allotment number will be published here once AMFI allots it.

Q: How do I lodge a grievance?

A: See the Grievance Redressal page: Level 1 support desk response within 48 working hours, Level 2 Compliance Officer escalation within 7 working days, followed by SEBI SCORES 2.0 and SMART ODR.

Q: Is Tourism open for commercial bookings?

A: No. It is an upcoming preview with an Early Access Waitlist; no bookings, reservations, or payments are accepted.

Q: What happens to my form submission details?

A: Your details are used strictly to respond to your explicit service inquiry. See our Privacy Policy for full details under the DPDP Act, 2023.

Investor Charter in Respect of Mutual Funds

A plain-language summary of investor rights and responsibilities in mutual fund transactions, drawn from SEBI and AMFI investor-education material. Refer to SEBI and the AMC for the full Investor Charter text.

A. Vision & Mission

Vision: To facilitate transparent, disciplined, and informed participation of investors in securities markets.
Mission: To provide transparent distribution of mutual fund schemes, ensure timely and accurate dissemination of scheme documents, and protect investor interests against fraudulent practices.

B. Rights of Investors

  • Receive transparent information about scheme objectives, asset allocation, benchmark, riskometer, and expense ratio.
  • Receive account statements and Consolidated Account Statements (CAS) as prescribed by SEBI.
  • Receive IDCW (Income Distribution cum Capital Withdrawal) payouts or redemption proceeds within SEBI timelines.
  • Access an independent and time-bound grievance redressal mechanism.

C. Do's and Don'ts for Investors

DO's

  • Read Key Information Memorandum (KIM) and Scheme Information Document (SID) before investing.
  • Verify the ARN validity of your distributor on AMFI India.
  • Make payments only via official banking channels directly to the AMC or recognized clearing corporation.
  • Keep KYC and contact credentials updated with RTAs.

DON'Ts

  • Do NOT pay cash to any distributor, agent, or representative.
  • Do NOT sign blank transaction slips or surrender checkbooks.
  • Do NOT be swayed by guaranteed return claims. No mutual fund or equity instrument can legally guarantee returns.
  • Do NOT share Demat passwords or OTPs with third parties.

D. Responsibilities of Investors

  • Provide complete and accurate KYC credentials and update changes promptly.
  • Read the Scheme Information Document (SID) and Key Information Memorandum (KIM) before investing.
  • Verify the distributor's ARN registration status on the AMFI India portal.
  • Make payments only via official banking channels directly to the AMC or clearing corporation (BSE StAR MF / NSE NMF II).
  • Review account statements, contract notes, and CAS carefully and report discrepancies promptly.
  • Keep contact details (email and mobile) current to receive direct transaction alerts from AMCs and RTAs.

Standard Risk Disclosures & Disclaimer

Mandatory investor disclosures under Securities and Exchange Board of India and Association of Mutual Funds in India regulations.

1. Mutual Fund Investments

Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks, read all scheme related documents carefully. Past performance of the schemes or fund managers is not an indicator of future performance. There is no assurance or guarantee that the objective of the mutual fund scheme will be achieved. Net Asset Value (NAV) of the units may fluctuate based on interest rates, equity market conditions, and macroeconomic variables.

2. Alternative Investment Funds (AIF) & PMS

Investments in PMS and Alternative Investment Funds involve a high degree of risk. Portfolios may be concentrated; no return is assured; management and performance fees apply regardless of performance. These products are intended only for eligible investors who possess the financial capacity to bear substantial risks, including illiquidity and potential capital loss. Units of close-ended AIFs cannot be liquidated prior to fund maturity.

2A. Bonds, NCDs and Section 54EC Bonds

Debt instruments carry credit risk (the issuer may delay or default), interest-rate risk, and liquidity risk. Credit ratings can be revised or withdrawn. Section 54EC capital gains bonds are locked in for five years, cannot be transferred or pledged, are not sovereign-guaranteed, and pay taxable interest.

2B. Market-Linked Debentures and Structured Notes

Redemption value depends on a market reference and on the issuer's ability to pay. Principal protection, where offered, applies only at maturity and only if the issuer meets its obligations. Secondary-market liquidity may be limited or absent.

2C. Specialised Investment Funds (SIF)

SIF strategies may use derivatives, including short positions, and can lose value faster than conventional mutual fund schemes. Minimum investment threshold of ₹10 Lakhs applies. Redemption terms are scheme-specific as set out in each Scheme Information Document (SID).

3. Commission Disclosure

Commission earned by IRAH Services LLP from AMCs on the schemes it distributes is disclosed to investors on request, in line with applicable SEBI and AMFI requirements. Details are provided in writing upon inquiry.
